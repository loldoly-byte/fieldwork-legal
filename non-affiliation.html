Required placement

  • Put the short notice in the Apple and Google long descriptions immediately after the

feature copy, not behind a website link.

  • Keep the full notice persistently reachable from Settings and About.
  • Include the full notice in the Terms of Use or linked legal page.
  • Show an independence statement near any in-app exam selector where a certification

body name could otherwise imply a relationship.

  • Do not put a certification-body name in the app name, subtitle, icon, developer name,

or feature graphic. Use it only where needed to describe supported exam content.

Visual and copy guardrails

  • No certification-body logos, wordmarks, seals, badges, or copied icon shapes.
  • No certification-body palette, typography, layout system, or other trade-dress mimicry.
  • Never use “official,” “approved,” “authorized,” “partner,” “endorsed,” or “sponsored”

except in the negative disclaimer.

  • Do not use proprietary model names such as “OPT” in the app name, icon, or branding.
  • Do not imply that buying or completing the app grants certification or exam entry.
  • Use original screenshots and artwork only; third-party citations should appear as

plain source text, not publisher or certifier logos.
