CPT Prep: Personal Trainer Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-15 Updated: 2026-10-19 Operator: Trinity Fallow

Summary

CPT Prep: Personal Trainer works without an account. Detailed answers and study history are stored on your device. If you allow optional usage analytics, Google Analytics for Firebase receives the limited usage information described below. When you buy or restore paid access, the app sends the store receipt or purchase token to our verification service to validate entitlement and prevent purchase fraud. We do not display third-party advertising or request an advertising identifier.

Information stored on your device

The app stores information needed to provide its study features, which may include:

  • study progress and review schedules;
  • answers, confidence ratings, response timing, and mock-exam results;
  • bookmarks, preferences, readiness evidence, and subscription access state; and
  • downloaded or bundled study content and local technical settings.

These detailed study records are processed locally. We cannot view, retrieve, or restore them because the app does not provide an account or cloud-sync service.

The information remains until it is removed through an available device or app-data control, or the app is uninstalled. Your operating-system backup service may include app data according to your device settings and the release build's backup configuration.

Purchases

Subscriptions are offered through Apple's App Store or Google Play. The store processes payment information under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details.

The app sends the product identifier, store source, and opaque receipt or purchase token to an operator-controlled HTTPS verification service. The service validates the purchase with Apple or Google and returns whether paid access is active. It does not receive your study answers or study history. Network infrastructure may process an IP address and standard security logs when handling the request.

We use purchase information only to provide and restore paid access, reconcile expiry, refunds, holds, or revocations, prevent fraud, and meet legal or accounting duties. Raw receipt or token data must not be retained longer than needed for verification and reconciliation; transaction records may be retained where required by law.

You can manage or cancel a subscription using the controls provided by the store where you purchased it.

Optional usage analytics

You can choose whether to share usage analytics before collection begins and change your choice in Settings. Declining does not restrict study features or purchases. We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand onboarding progress, whether people begin and finish study, return on later days, reach paid-access screens, and complete purchases. We may link our Analytics property to our Google Ads account to measure campaign results, with personalized advertising disabled.

If you allow this, the app sends event names, the selected certification exam, paywall entry point, product and plan identifiers, and activity/cohort dates. It also records the first completed study activity for the current analytics installation. It does not upload questions, selected answers, correctness, individual scores, confidence ratings, your scheduled exam date, issue notes or other free text. Receipt/token verification remains separate from these custom analytics events.

Google's SDK also collects standard app and device information, app-instance identifiers, usage events and purchase events. Google processes IP addresses to derive approximate location. We disable advertising-ID collection and deny advertising storage, advertising user-data and advertising-personalization consent in the app. We do not set a user account ID, enable Google signals, or use this analytics to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Google processes analytics information on its infrastructure, which may be outside your country. See Google's privacy policy and Firebase privacy information. Analytics reports are pseudonymous, not guaranteed anonymous. A study cohort is measured from the start of optional analytics, which may be after installation.

Turning analytics off stops future collection and resets the analytics identifier and analytics state on this device. This does not itself erase information already received by Google. Contact trinityfallow@gmail.com for questions or deletion requests; we may need additional information to identify a relevant record, and may be unable to identify your prior pseudonymous events after an identifier reset. The Analytics property is configured to retain event-level and user data for two months, without resetting that period on new user activity. Aggregated reports may remain longer.

Support communications

If you contact us outside the app, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond, investigate the issue, protect the service, and meet legal obligations. Do not send health records, payment-card details, passwords, or recalled questions from a certification exam.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes, then delete or de-identify it unless a longer period is required by law.

Information we do not collect through the app

Under the release design covered by this policy, we do not collect through the app:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account identifiers;
  • precise location;
  • health records or personal fitness measurements;
  • contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or files;
  • advertising identifiers or device fingerprints;
  • detailed study answers, search history, or crash reports off the device; or
  • content for advertising, sale, or profiling.

The app contains educational material about health and fitness topics. Viewing or answering questions about those topics does not cause the app to collect your personal health or fitness information.

Sharing, sale, advertising, and tracking

We do not sell personal information. Optional app usage data is processed by Google Analytics as described above. We do not provide study answers to advertisers or data brokers. We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and the app does not display third-party advertising.

Apple and Google may process information independently when they operate their stores, devices, platform services, and purchase systems. Their processing is governed by their own notices and settings.

Your choices

Because detailed study data stays on your device, we cannot access, export, correct, or delete it for you. You can remove local data using any data-reset control included in the release or through your device's app-storage controls; uninstalling the app ordinarily removes its active local data, subject to platform backup and restore behavior.

You can ask us to delete support correspondence or exercise privacy rights that apply where you live by contacting trinityfallow@gmail.com. We may need enough information to locate the correspondence and verify the request. Mandatory legal retention may still apply. The same contact can be used for questions about operator-held purchase-verification records, although the App Store or Google Play controls the underlying store account and payment record.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 16 and older and is not directed to children under 16. If you believe a child has sent personal information through a support channel, contact trinityfallow@gmail.com.

Security

Local data is protected by the security features of your device and operating system. Purchase-verification requests use HTTPS encryption in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your device updated and protect access with an appropriate passcode or biometric control.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. We will publish the new effective date and provide any additional notice required by law. We will update the store privacy disclosures before a release introduces new data practices.
