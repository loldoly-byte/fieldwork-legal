Fieldwork Auto-Renewing Subscription Terms

Effective: 2026-08-04 Provider: Fieldwork

Subscription options

The app may offer these durations for the same paid-access tier:

  • Monthly: renews every one month.
  • 90-day: configured and billed by the store as a three-month subscription.
  • Annual: renews every one year.

The exact price and currency are the localized values shown beside each option and in the Apple or Google purchase-confirmation sheet. Taxes may be handled according to the store and your location. Do not rely on a price shown outside the live purchase flow.

What paid access includes

While the subscription is active, paid access unlocks the content and features described on the paywall at purchase time. The free study package remains usable without a paid subscription. A free package is not a free trial and does not convert to a paid plan by itself.

Payment and automatic renewal

Payment is charged to the store account you confirm at purchase. The subscription automatically renews for the same duration at the then-disclosed store price unless you cancel before the next renewal is processed. The store controls billing timing, authorization, receipts, taxes, and renewal processing.

If an introductory offer or free trial is added later, its eligibility, duration, post-offer price, and conversion date must be displayed before purchase and added to these Terms. No introductory offer or trial is covered by this draft.

Cancellation and access after cancellation

Cancel through the same store used for purchase:

  • Manage Apple subscriptions
  • Manage Google Play subscriptions

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Unless the store or applicable law provides otherwise, cancellation stops future renewal and paid access continues until the end of the already-paid billing period.

The app must provide an easy-to-find manage-subscription link in Settings or an equivalent screen before launch.

Restore purchases

Use Restore Purchases in the app to ask the applicable store to restore an eligible entitlement. Restoration depends on the store account, product status, and store service.

Price or benefit changes

The store and Fieldwork will provide any notice or consent flow required for a price change. Material changes to paid benefits will be described before they take effect, subject to applicable law and store rules.

Refunds

Refund requests are handled under the rules and process of the store where the purchase was made, without limiting any mandatory consumer right:

  • Request an Apple refund
  • Google Play refunds
