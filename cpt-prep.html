Before you install

A few useful details.

Which exams does CPT Prep support?

NASM and NCSF offer expanded supplemental practice. ACE, ACSM, NSCA and NCCPT currently focus on shared foundations, with incomplete certification-specific coverage. Each selected certification keeps its own plan, practice history and progress.

Is this an official certification app?

No. CPT Prep is an independent study tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by the certification bodies. Practice questions are independent study material, not official exam questions. Use it alongside your provider's current materials.

Is it available on iPhone?

This campaign is for the Android app on Google Play. There is no iPhone download offered on this page.

Does the starter package include everything?

The free starter package is limited. Optional Full Study Access unlocks the complete available library and full-length mock forms. Actual access and localized prices are shown inside the app before purchase.

Will it guarantee that I pass?

No. CPT Prep does not guarantee passing, certification or score improvement. Mock forms draw from the study bank and material may overlap across packs.
