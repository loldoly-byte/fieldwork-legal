Help test Fieldwork before it launches

Fieldwork is a study app for the NASM Certified Personal Trainer exam. It is finished and it needs twelve people to use it for two weeks before Google will let it go public.

If you are studying for the CPT, you get the full app free for taking part.

Who this is for

People actually preparing for the NASM-CPT — ideally with an exam date booked, or planning to sit it in the next few months. You do not need to be far into your study. Someone starting from scratch is as useful to test with as someone revising.

You need an Android phone and the Google account you use on it.

What taking part involves

  • Install the app from the link below and keep it installed for 14 days.
  • Do the daily session most days. It is one session a day, sized to the time

you pick — 10, 20 or 30 minutes — and it ends when it ends. There is no feed.

  • Tell me what is wrong with it. Confusing wording, a question you think is

incorrect, anything that annoyed you.

That is the whole commitment. Fourteen days, one short session a day, and your honest opinion.

Please stay opted in for the full 14 days. Google requires twelve testers opted in continuously; if someone drops out partway the count resets for everybody, and the two weeks starts again.

What you get

  • The complete app — all 149 concepts, all six exam domains, the full-length

mock exams — free, for good, for taking part.

  • No payment card, no account, no sign-in, no email address required by the

app itself.

What the app does

One session a day, built from three things: mistakes you got wrong while feeling confident, material scheduled to come back today, and the next concept you have not met. You recall each key rule from memory with the reading closed, and you rate your confidence *before* you see whether you were right — answers you were sure about and got wrong go to the front of the queue, because those are the ones ordinary studying leaves in place.

It works offline. Your study data stays on your device and is never uploaded.

Join

https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.ptstudy.pt_study

Open that link on your Android phone, signed in with the Google account you use for the Play Store, and accept the invitation. The app then installs from Play as normal.

If the link says you are not on the tester list, email me and I will add you.
